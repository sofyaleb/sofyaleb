### 🔬 Research

At the [Ansari Lab](https://www.ndm.ox.ac.uk/team/azim-ansari), I study **host–pathogen interactions in hepatitis C virus (HCV)**, which affects around 50 million people worldwide.

HCV infections vary widely between individuals because of both human genetic diversity and viral genomic variation. I characterise differences in viral **nucleotide content, codon usage and RNA structure** across genotypes, and link them to clinical outcomes using whole-genome alignments and regression modelling.

**Toolkit:** biostatistics · bioinformatics · genomics · immunology

I care about advancing research on neglected diseases and hope my work can inform policy.
